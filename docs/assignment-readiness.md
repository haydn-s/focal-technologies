## Presentation preparation

The team will create this next. Cover:

1. **Problem and evolution · at most two minutes:** the original problem, the target users, and what changed after discovery.
2. **Three hypotheses · about two minutes each:** each persona, the core flow, its difference from the others, and what the comparison can teach the team.
3. **Live demos · about two minutes each:** demonstrate the primary journey and a clean reset; avoid narrating every click.
4. **Prioritization and strategy:** value, effort, complexity, success signals, deliberate cuts, and what evidence would change the team's direction.

Present proposed hypotheses as hypotheses. Preference, clarity ratings, reminder intent, and download requests are not clinical validation or proof of later behavior. Fictional examples should stay labeled during demos.
