## Evidence and financial alignment

- [Customer discovery workbook](../docs/research/customer-discovery/interview-workbook.docx) and [presentation](../docs/research/customer-discovery/presentation.pptx): five real interviews, including one parent, and five AI persona interviews. The latter are hypothesis development, not customer validation.
- [Define phase workbook](../docs/assignments/define-phase-workbook.pdf), [prototype presentation](<../docs/presentations/Focal Prototype Presentation.pptx>) and the [three prototype guides](../README.md#choose-a-demo): product evolution and the selection of the family direction.
- [Competitive workbook](../docs/research/competitive-landscape/workbook.docx), [business-model presentation](../docs/strategy/business-model-presentation.pdf), [Lean Canvas](../docs/strategy/lean-canvas.pptx) and [brand assets](../assets/brand/README.md): strategy history, roles and identity. Earlier clinical, agency and subscription ideas are not presented as current capabilities or validated advantages.
- [Prototype B guide](../prototypes/prototype-b-parent-guided-check/README.md): current parent preparation, experimental task and history capabilities, including clinical and data limits.
- [Final executive pitch](<../docs/presentations/executive-pitch/Focal Family Executive Pitch - Final 12min.pptx>), [notes](../docs/presentations/executive-pitch/notes.json), [assumptions](../docs/presentations/executive-pitch/model.json) and [calculated model](../docs/presentations/executive-pitch/calculated-model.json): proposed school buyer, pricing, funnel, pilot, operating responsibilities, forecasts and funding terms.

The document keeps the $2,400 annual school license, $600 annual direct cost per site, $600,000 raise for 15% equity, and $3.4 million proposed pre-money valuation. It distinguishes recognized revenue from exit annual recurring revenue and free pilot sites from paid sites. School demand, conversion, retention, outcomes and partnerships remain proposed or unvalidated.
