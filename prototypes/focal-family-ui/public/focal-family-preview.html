Focal DMV readiness concept (secondary exploration)

Student prototype

Alongside Focal Family (designed for parents of children aged 4–10), our student team explored a secondary concept for adults preparing for an upcoming driver’s license vision renewal test at the DMV.

Why Focal Family is our primary focus:

Parents receiving a school vision screening letter often feel uncertain about what the letter means and what to share at an eye exam. Focal Family turns a calm at-home activity and parent observations into a concrete, editable note for a school nurse or pediatric eye doctor.
